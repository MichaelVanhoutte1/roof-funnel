Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.

  • Financial Information: Credit score range or financial qualification details you voluntarily submit during the application process.

Use and Processing of Collected Information

To ensure compliance with data privacy laws and to clearly communicate how personal information is processed, we provide the following details regarding the use and processing of your collected information.

We may use and process your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • To process your application for our dealer program.
  • To send you marketing and promotional communications, including via email, SMS, and social media advertising platforms (you may opt out at any time).
  • To personalize your experience and deliver content and product offerings relevant to your interests.
  • To improve our website, products, and services through analytics and usage data.
  • To create and manage your account and maintain our business relationship with you.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.
  • To share information with advertising platforms (such as Meta / Facebook) for the purpose of delivering targeted advertisements, measuring ad performance, and optimizing marketing campaigns. This may include sharing hashed or unhashed contact information (e.g., name, email, phone number) through integrations such as the Meta Conversions API (CAPI) or Meta Pixel.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, web beacons, tracking pixels (including the Meta Pixel), and similar technologies to collect usage data, remember your preferences, and deliver relevant advertising. These technologies help us understand how visitors interact with our website and measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns.

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

  • Advertising Partners: Platforms like Meta (Facebook/Instagram) for ad targeting, measurement, and optimization through tools such as the Meta Pixel and Conversions API.
  • Analytics Providers: Third-party analytics services that help us analyze website traffic and usage patterns.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. When your information is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymize it.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected, the purposes for collection, and the categories of third parties with whom we share your information. You also have the right to request deletion of your personal information and to not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 18, we will promptly delete it.

Data Security
